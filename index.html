      What does the free check actually do?

      It checks your website's address in your own browser: whether it uses a secure HTTPS connection, whether the address is short and clean, and whether the site responds. Then the 8-question self-audit covers the things only a human looking at the site can judge — mobile-friendliness, speed, Google presence, contact details and more. It does not crawl your pages or access anything private, and your answers stay on your device.

      Starter audit reports are delivered within 3 working days. Growth usually takes 1–2 weeks, depending on access to your website. Business runs over about 4 weeks, because it includes a month of content and social media help plus a final results report.
