# Website Booster

Free website check for small businesses in Pakistan — by Dr. S. Ahmad.

**Live:** https://drsahmad.github.io/website-booster/

## What it does

- **Step 1 — Free check:** enter a website address. It checks HTTPS, address cleanliness and whether the site answers, right in the visitor's browser.
- **Step 2 — Self-audit:** 8 honest questions (mobile, speed, Google Business Profile, meta, contact, fresh content, social, analytics) with a weighted score out of 100, a gauge, a grade, a breakdown by area, a personal boost checklist and a 30-day plan.
- **Done-for-you packages:** Starter PKR 4,999 · Growth PKR 9,999 · Business PKR 19,999. Ordering opens an enquiry by email — no online payment.

## Honest by design

The free check looks at the address and whether the site answers. It does not crawl or scan pages — no free instant tool honestly can. The self-audit covers what needs human eyes. Together they give a fair score.

## Use

Single self-contained file — open `index.html` in any browser, or host it anywhere. No build step, no dependencies, no sign-up, no data leaves the visitor's device. To change the enquiry email, replace `nobleinterestinternational@gmail.com` in the file.
